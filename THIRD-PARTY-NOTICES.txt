Tabler Icons
------------

Component: Tabler Icons, outline style
Version:   tag v3.49.0, commit bbed884d15354b5cebf2493371f20dc2d5e83eaf
Source:    https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/tree/bbed884d15354b5cebf2493371f20dc2d5e83eaf
Licence:   MIT, reproduced below exactly as published at that tag
Used for:  NextSpace Teacher Console action and control icons (toolbar, menus, computer-tile action menu, view strip, grid controls, Screenshots, Slideshow and Spotlight panels)

Imported glyphs (38 files from icons/outline/ at the commit above):
  align-box-center-middle, app-window, apps, camera, device-desktop, device-desktop-minus,
  device-desktop-plus, device-floppy, devices-pc, dots, eye, file-download, file-upload,
  folder-open, info-circle, lamp-2, layout-grid, lock, lock-open, login, logout, maximize, message,
  pencil, player-pause, player-play, player-skip-back, player-skip-forward, pointer, power, refresh,
  refresh-off, screen-share, slideshow, trash, users, world, zoom-scan

Local modification. The imported source files are unmodified; the product draws path data generated from them.
  Stroke width: the upstream stroke of 2.0 units on the 24-unit grid is drawn at 2.1 units, which is
  1.75 logical pixels at the 20-logical-pixel action-icon size. Every glyph keeps its upstream geometry.
Shared treatments (one upstream glyph reused for several actions; these are not distinct glyphs):
  camera is shared by Screenshot and the Screenshots-panel toggle.
  power is shared by Power On, Power Down and the powered-on filter.
  eye is shared by Remote View and the Screenshots-panel Show button.
  maximize is shared by the own-screen and selected-user fullscreen share modes and the Slideshow
    separate-window toggle.
  app-window is shared by the own-screen and selected-user window share modes.
  refresh is shared by Reboot and the Spotlight-panel realtime toggle (its checked On state).
